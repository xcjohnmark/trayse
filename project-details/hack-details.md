## 3. Product Categories / Verticals in Colosseum Arena

When tagging and building your product on the [Colosseum Arena](https://colosseum.com/arena), you classify under:
* **DeFi:** DEXs, automated market makers, perps, lending, liquid staking, yield vaults.
* **DePIN:** Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks (sensor networks, compute, mapping, wireless).
* **Payments:** Remittances, agentic AI micro-payments, merchant rails, streaming finance.
* **Consumer:** Social, messaging, content monetization, creator tokens, on-chain identity.
* **Developer Infrastructure:** Tooling, SDKs, indexing, security, RPC optimization.
* **Gaming:** Verifiable game state, on-chain mechanics, interoperable game assets.
* **AI & Agentic Systems:** Autonomous on-chain agents, verifiable inference, AI payment routing.
* **Data & Analytics:** On-chain indexing, risk simulation, market intelligence.

## 4. The Colosseum Accelerator Program

Winning or placing high in the hackathon fast-tracks your team to the **Colosseum Accelerator**, the most selective launchpad in crypto (~0.7% acceptance rate).

* **The Investment:** **$250,000 pre-seed check** (SAFE + Token Warrant, or Colosseum STAMP for MetaDAO tokens).
* **Solana Requirement:** Even though this hackathon has multi-chain tracks, **acceptance into the Accelerator strictly requires integration with the Solana ecosystem**.
* **12-Week Program Structure:**
  * **Weeks 1–2 (In-Person):** Mandatory residency in **San Francisco** at Colosseum’s Financial District headquarters (meals, office space, 24/7 access provided).
  * **Weeks 3–11 (Hybrid / Remote):** Build from anywhere in the world (SF office remains open).
  * **Week 12 (In-Person):** San Francisco **Demo Day** presenting live before **150+ leading venture capital firms** (Multicoin, Pantera, Foundation Capital, Solana Ventures).
* **Mentorship:** 1:1 advisory with Colosseum co-founders (Matty Taylor, Clay Robbins, Nate Levine) and guest sessions with ecosystem leaders (Anatoly Yakovenko, Raj Gokal, Mert Mumtaz).
* **Visas:** Colosseum provides immigration and US visa referral support for international founders.

## 5. Submission Requirements & Rules

To pass initial screening, you must submit before **October 12, 2026, 11:59 PM PDT** with the following assets:

1. **GitHub Repository:**
   * Public repository preferred.
   * If private, you **must invite `hackathon@colosseum.com`** before the deadline.
   * **Commit History:** Judges review your Git log. Code must clearly reflect substantial commits made during the hackathon window.
2. **Pitch Presentation Video (2–3 minutes):**
   * Covers: Problem, Solution, Market Size (TAM), Business Model, and Team Background.
3. **Live Product Demo & Walkthrough Video (up to 3 minutes):**
   * Video showing the actual software running.
   * A functional web/mobile app link deployed on **Devnet, Testnet, or Mainnet**.
4. **Team & Traction Data:**
   * All team members must be registered on Colosseum Arena and linked to the project.
   * Strict rule: **Only 1 project submission per person/team.**
5. **New vs. Existing Code Policy:**
   * Designed for early-stage startups that have not raised institutional capital.
   * You may continue an existing idea, but **only work completed during the hackathon timeline is judged**. You must disclose pre-existing code upfront in the submission portal.

## 6. How Judging Works

Colosseum evaluates entries as **fundable startups**, not school hackathon prototypes:

1. **Founder + Market Fit:** Does your team have the domain expertise and grit to build full-time?
2. **Product Execution:** How polished, functional, and fast is the software?
3. **Unique Insight:** Are you solving a genuine friction or leveraging a new capability (e.g. SVM speed, compression, agentic rails)?
4. **Viability & Business Model:** Can this grow into a venture-backed protocol or company?
5. **Finalist Interviews:** Top-tier projects undergo a **15-minute live Zoom interview** with the Colosseum team before final prizes and accelerator invitations are locked.

## 7. Step-by-Step: How to Register & Submit

1. **Sign Up:** Create your profile at [colosseum.com/arena/hackathon/register](https://colosseum.com/arena/hackathon/register).
2. **Find Teammates (Optional):** Use the built-in [Co-founder Matching portal](https://colosseum.com/cofounder-matching) or the Colosseum Discord.
3. **Create Your Project Draft:** Go to your Arena dashboard, click **Create Project**, select your primary track and category, and add your team members.
4. **Build & Ship:** Keep commits active on your repository. You can post 1-minute weekly video updates on Arena to get on the radar of Colosseum partners early.
5. **Submit by October 12, 11:59 PM PDT:** Double-check that your repo permissions are open to `hackathon@colosseum.com`, your demo video is public/unlisted, and your live URL works without crashes.
