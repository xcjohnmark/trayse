# Trayse — Product & Company Overview

## 1. Company & Product Definition

### What Trayse Is
**Trayse** is a quantitative intelligence, analytics, and strategy platform built specifically for prediction markets. It combines institutional market intelligence, multi-modal AI reasoning, and quantitative strategy automation on top of Solana.

### Mission
To bridge institutional quantitative finance with modern prediction markets. Trayse allows novices to navigate markets with proven quantitative strategies, copytrade smart-money whales, and access arbitrage insights, while giving experienced quants structured market data, model-building kits, and trustless on-chain monetization tools.

---

## 2. Core Classifications & Features

### Classification A: Market Intelligence & Analytics
*The real-time data foundation of the terminal.*
* **Cross-Platform Market Browser:** Normalized live odds, volumes, order book depth, and closing schedules across major prediction venues.
* **Unified Watchlists:** Custom monitoring dashboards organized by asset class, macro events, politics, and real-world indicators.
* **Arbitrage Scanner:** Real-time detection of price discrepancies and synthetic risk-free spreads across fragmented markets.
* **Trade Journal & Notes:** Automated personal position tracking, trade rationale notes, and historical audit exports.

### Classification B: Quantitative Research & Strategy Studio
*The mathematical and algorithmic engine.*
* **Quant Indicator Models:** Native integration of verified mathematical models (weather systems, economic nowcasts, polling data) against live market probabilities.
* **Edge & Mispricing Engine:** Automated calculation of statistical spread between external model probability and market-implied odds.
* **Backtesting & Simulation Harness:** Historical event data environment to test model performance before committing capital.
* **Developer SDK:** Clean programmatic library (`trayse-sdk`) for external quants to plug custom algorithmic strategies into the platform.

### Classification C: Solana Capital & Execution Infrastructure
*The high-speed, non-custodial financial layer.*
* **Web3 Wallet Hub:** Native connection for Solana wallets (Phantom, Backpack, Solflare).
* **Yield-Bearing Margin Collateral:** Integration with interest-bearing dollar stablecoins so capital locked in pending limit orders or long-duration events earns continuous yield.
* **Delegated Session Keys & Risk Controls:** Smart-wallet session keys allowing automated bots to execute within strict, user-defined rules (hard stop-losses, slippage caps, auto-expiry).
* **On-Chain Strategy Vaults:** Smart-contract vaults where users deposit funds to follow quantitative strategies with transparent, automated profit sharing.

### Classification D: Social Alpha & Copytrading
*The community engagement and whale tracking layer.*
* **Whale Radar:** Real-time on-chain transaction monitoring identifying high-conviction trades from top-tier prediction wallets.
* **Trader Leaderboard & Profiles:** Verified performance rankings (30-day PnL, win rates, drawdown, category specialization).
* **Automated Copytrading Engine:** Proportional execution mimicking verified leader wallets with custom allocation limits.
* **Solana Actions & Blinks:** Shareable links that unfurl live market charts and one-click betting actions directly inside Twitter/X and Discord feeds.

### Classification E: Autonomous AI & Reasoning Engine
*The contextual interpretation layer.*
* **AI Market Copilot:** Natural language synthesis explaining *why* odds are mispriced based on news, data releases, and order flow.
* **Sentiment & Narrative Aggregator:** Multi-source sentiment analysis scoring public consensus against market pricing.
* **x402 Agentic Micropayments:** Standardized machine-to-machine payment protocol allowing external AI agents to purchase Trayse quant signals per query.

## 3. Product Roadmap (From 1 to N)

A progressive build sequence moving from the core data and quant foundations to the complete institutional platform:

### 1. Unified Market Ingestion & Terminal UI
* **Classification:** Market Intelligence & Analytics
* **Functionality:** Aggregates and displays live prediction markets in a high-density, dark-mode terminal.
* **Features Involved:** Cross-platform market browser, live odds, order book depth, basic search and filtering.

### 2. Quantitative Model Engine & Edge Calculator
* **Classification:** Quantitative Research & Strategy Studio
* **Functionality:** Runs mathematical predictive models and computes real-time pricing edges against market odds.
* **Features Involved:** Automated probability calculation, model vs. market spread indicator, mispricing alert feed.

### 3. Solana Wallet Hub & Session Key Guardrails
* **Classification:** Solana Capital & Execution Infrastructure
* **Functionality:** Authenticates users via Solana wallets and establishes delegated execution boundaries.
* **Features Involved:** Phantom/Backpack wallet adapter, scoped session keys, max position limits, hard daily loss caps.

### 4. Solana Strategy Vaults & Yield-Bearing Margin
* **Classification:** Solana Capital & Execution Infrastructure
* **Functionality:** Allows users to stake capital into automated quant strategies while earning yield on idle margin.
* **Features Involved:** Non-custodial strategy deposit vaults, yield-bearing stablecoin collateral, automated profit-share logic.

### 5. On-Chain Whale Radar & Leaderboard
* **Classification:** Social Alpha & Copytrading
* **Functionality:** Indexes smart-money prediction wallets and ranks top performers publicly.
* **Features Involved:** Whale transaction monitor, verified trader profiles, 30-day PnL leaderboard, smart-money alert stream.

### 6. Automated Copytrading Bot
* **Classification:** Social Alpha & Copytrading
* **Functionality:** Allows users to mirror leader wallets or quant strategies automatically.
* **Features Involved:** Proportional trade sizing, slippage protection, execution logs, personal portfolio tracker.

### 7. Autonomous AI Market Copilot
* **Classification:** Autonomous AI & Reasoning Engine
* **Functionality:** Generates natural language analysis explaining market conditions and news drivers.
* **Features Involved:** Contextual event summaries, sentiment scoring, AI-assisted trade rationale.

### 8. Cross-Platform Arbitrage Scanner
* **Classification:** Market Intelligence & Analytics
* **Functionality:** Identifies synthetic risk-free and directional arbitrage opportunities across venues.
* **Features Involved:** Spread detector, multi-market odds calculator, instant arbitrage notification stream.

### 9. Solana Actions & Blinks (Viral Social Trading)
* **Classification:** Social Alpha & Copytrading
* **Functionality:** Turns Trayse market intelligence and copytrade signals into interactive social cards.
* **Features Involved:** Dialect Blinks endpoint, 1-click Twitter/X betting buttons, shareable alpha cards.

### 10. Developer SDK & Agentic Micropayments (Milestone N)
* **Classification:** Quantitative Research & Autonomous AI
* **Functionality:** Full programmatic platform enabling external quants and autonomous AI agents to build and monetize models.
* **Features Involved:** Python `trayse-sdk`, backtesting datasets, x402 pay-per-query API, open strategy marketplace.
